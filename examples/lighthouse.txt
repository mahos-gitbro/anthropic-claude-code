Every night for forty years, old Marta climbed the lighthouse stairs and lit the lamp. The town said ships used GPS now and the light was useless. One stormy night the power failed across the whole coast. Marta's grandson Leo, visiting for the summer, found her already halfway up the stairs with an oil lantern. "Help me carry this," she said. Together they hauled the lantern to the top and lit the old wick behind the great lens. Far out at sea, a fishing boat that had lost all its instruments saw the beam cut through the rain and turned for home. In the morning the captain came up the hill with a basket of fish and said nothing, only bowed. Leo looked at his grandmother. "Same time tonight?" he asked. She smiled.
